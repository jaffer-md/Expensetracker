Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we also store the expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to authenticate you, to record and display your expenses, and to generate summaries and filters of your spending. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Passwords are never stored in plain text. While we work to keep your information secure, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact the Spendly team at support@spendly.example.
